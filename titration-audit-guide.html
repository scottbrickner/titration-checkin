Titration Audit Guide

Everything an auditor needs for the vasoactive and propofol titration audit in one place: when to audit, how to score each drip, how to coach, and how to enter it in the Titration Audit Tool.

!

Audit the policy, not your practice.

If the chart meets policy, it's compliant, even if your unit does more. Unit expectations go in Comments, never in a "No."

Before you audit

The audit checks whether nurses titrated vasoactive and propofol infusions the way the provider ordered. It's also how non-compliance gets coached and corrected in real time.

What counts as a finding

Vasoactive infusions

Did the RN titrate by the ordered dose, no more often than the ordered frequency?

Example: order says titrate by 2 mcg/min q5 min. Every change should be 2 mcg/min or less, at least 5 minutes apart.

Propofol infusions

Did the RN titrate by the ordered dose, no more often than the ordered frequency?

A supporting RASS with each titration is a Practice point, and a potential finding. Check it every time and coach any gap.

Example: titrate by 5 mcg/kg/min q10 min to RASS −2. Changes no more often than q10 min, each with a documented RASS.

Logistics

Who audits
Managers, ANMs, Nurse Leads, Relief Nurse Leads and SWAT RNs who have been validated by a CNS. Only validated, calibrated auditors' entries count.
How often
Once per shift.
When
No sooner than 6 hours into the shift: 1300 or later on days, 0100 or later on nights. Your unit can pick a set time that meets this.
Review period
Drip documentation from the current shift only. Audit while the nurse is still working. Retroactive audits defeat the purpose: errors are coached and corrected in real time.
Which drips
Vasoactive drips with titration orders, and all propofol drips. Don't audit a drip that wasn't on at any point during the review period.
Where it goes
The Titration Audit Tool in the Nurse Portal, through the Forms link. Never add entries in the list or grid view. A paper tool is fine as a worksheet, but final results go in the electronic tool.
Helpful report
Discern has a Titration Audit Report. Using it is optional.
Stuck on a scenario
Complete the audit as best you can, then escalate to your unit's CNS.

Three levels of scope

Finding
Titrated per order (dose and frequency). Required, and the measure this audit reports.
Practice point
Emergent titration, block charting, parameter documented before titration, 30-minute reassessment, volume. Audit and coach for the overall process. Not scored as a finding on their own.
Teaching only
Propofol orange lock box, port-less tubing. Coach at the bedside. Not scored toward compliance.

After a finding

1. Titration audit finding
Dose or frequency outside the order, without complete emergent block charting.
↓
2. Just-in-time coaching
Same shift, face to face, with teach-back. On the form: No – Coached and corrected in real time, plus Coached RN Name and Coached By.
↓
If you can't coach and correct in real time (the RN doesn't understand the error, or fails the teach-back): the RN comes off the assignment and can't care for patients on vasoactive or propofol infusions until they demonstrate competence. Tell the charge nurse so patients get reassigned.
↓
3. NPD debrief
Automatic. Selecting "Coached and corrected in real time" and entering the Coached RN Name sends the RN to the NPDNE Debriefs queue for follow-up education.
↓
4. Manager follow-up
After nursing education remediation, any further non-compliance goes through progressive discipline. Pending The definition of "repeat" is awaiting a leadership decision.

Enter the audit

Use the Forms link for every submission. Entering directly in the list or grid view skips the form's branching and leaves required fields blank. Question numbers change as the form branches.

  1. Start the audit. Select your unit and choose Titratable Drips Audit. Pick the pathway that fits:
    • Vasoactive drip: one entry per drip. This pathway also lets you add a propofol audit.
    • Propofol only: patient on propofol with no vasoactive titration orders.
    • There are no titratable drips on my unit today: then finish the remaining prompts.
  2. Date, time, patient. Enter the date and time the audit was performed, in four-digit military time without a colon (0730, 1900). If you're entering later from a worksheet, use the original audit time, not the submission time. Enter the patient's name in the Patient Name field, plus the room number, and verify it against the patient record. Don't substitute initials or a partial FIN. Never put patient identifiers in Comments or the coaching fields.
  3. Each vasoactive infusion. Select the drug, compare every documented change with the order in effect at the time, and answer Titrated per provider order – frequency & dose? If you answer No, the block charting question appears.
  4. Volume and next bag. VTBI remaining on the pump in mL. Time until the next bag is hung as a number of hours, not a clock time, based on the current rate and regardless of reason. If the drip was on earlier but isn't infusing now, enter 999.
  5. Another drip? Answer accurately. If Yes, complete the next section. Keep each drug's findings, volume and time together in its own section.
  6. Propofol questions (when shown). Titration frequency and dose against the order; block charting separately if it appears. Lock box and port-less tubing are by direct observation, each recorded on its own.
  7. Findings and coaching. Select Coached and corrected in real time only when coaching happened and the issue was corrected during the audit.
  8. Check, then submit. Unit, patient, date, time and every infusion entry correct; every displayed question answered; coaching names complete (or both blank). Submit and confirm it was accepted.
